🌓 Light & Dark Mode

A beautiful theme switcher that lets you toggle between light and dark modes. Your preference is saved automatically and persists across page visits.

☀️ Light Mode

🎯 How to Use

  1. Look for the theme toggle button (☀️/🌙) in the header (top-right)
  2. Click the toggle to switch between light and dark modes
  3. Watch the smooth transition as colors change instantly
  4. Your preference is saved automatically in browser storage
  5. Navigate to any page — the theme persists!
💾

Persistent Storage

Your theme choice is saved to localStorage and remembers your preference across sessions.

Instant Apply

Theme loads before page renders — no flash of wrong theme. Smooth 300ms transition.
